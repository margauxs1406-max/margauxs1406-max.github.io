Sourire

Your collection of happy memories. Private, offline and ad-free.

Good moments fade fast. Sourire keeps them: a word, a photo, ten seconds, and the memory is in the jar. On grey days, it hands one back to you at random.

What the app does

  • Collect: a memory takes seconds to write, with or without a photo. A notification can remind you, as often as you choose.
  • Draw at random: tap the jar and up comes a memory you had forgotten.
  • Unlock badges: from your first memory to the title of Legend of Sourire.
  • Share: send a memory to someone close, by message, by email or through your social apps, whenever you decide to.
  • Make it yours: a different decor for each note, and the freedom to reopen a memory and edit it.
  • Sort and find: categories, and a filter by month, year and category.

Your memories stay with you

No account, no cloud, no advertising. Everything is saved on your phone and nowhere else. The app works offline, and nothing ever leaves on its own: a memory only goes out of the jar if you send it yourself. The details are in the privacy policy.
